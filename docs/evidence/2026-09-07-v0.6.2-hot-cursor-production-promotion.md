## Reproduced scheduler failure

Immediately before the upgrade at `2026-09-07T12:38:25Z`, v0.6.1 reported one hot poll, last performed at
`2026-09-07T11:56:41.143Z`. The quote queue had fallen to 103 candidates and 132 event-selected candidates had already
been requoted, but the hot cursor remained `56605606` while the chain head was `56840105`: a lag of 234,499 blocks with
no provider error. This confirms that successful quote work, process health and a moving backlog did not imply current
event discovery.

## Still open

- The hot cursor remains 232,258 blocks behind the sampled head. Backlog no longer bypasses the bounded poll attempt,
  but provider success, current-head convergence and steady-state event-to-quote latency remain unproven.
- LONG, Doppler, target-pool and PAIR chain-catalog coverage remain partial. Adapter success is not whole-chain coverage.
- The public RPC again required bounded individual-call fallback after a malformed batch response. Current source health
  is successful, but provider efficiency remains degraded.
- Four current rows were proxy-positive only. A fresh exact preflight, explicit bounded signer authorization, canonical
  receipt and post-state are still required before any live-profit claim.
- Cold startup briefly exceeded `MemoryHigh` without reaching `MemoryMax`; longer-running pressure and disk-growth
  observation remain operational follow-up evidence.
