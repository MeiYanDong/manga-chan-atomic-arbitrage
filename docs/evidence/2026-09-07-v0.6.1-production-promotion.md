Normal source progress is one 50,000-block batch per periodic cycle. To verify the known NINECAT launch without waiting
for sixteen cycles, the board-only source batch count was temporarily set to 16. The original protected env was copied
to a mode-0600 backup, only `manga-opportunity-board.service` was restarted, and the three independent source cursors
advanced together from `45150000` to `45950000`. That process peaked at 249,495,552 bytes with no pressure, max or OOM
events.

The original env was then restored byte-for-byte to SHA-256
`3becb5520660c4046813c79e8b55fefca29f90140a480359401d6b9eaab0062e`; the temporary backup was removed. After the
board-only restart, the next periodic cycle advanced exactly one range to `46000000`, proving the temporary override
was gone. It peaked at 234,897,408 bytes with all memory-event counters zero.
